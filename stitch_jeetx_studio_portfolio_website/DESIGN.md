## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a luxury technical aesthetic tailored for elite web engineering and bespoke digital craft. It rejects the hyper-saturated, chaotic tropes of web3/cyberpunk in favor of restrained obsidian minimalism, crisp precision, and refined luminescence. 

### Core Ethos
- **Precision Engineering:** Clean layouts and balanced structural grids inspired by high-end developer tooling and Swiss typography.
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Rich, pitch-dark obsidian and deep violet surfaces that frame work like an exclusive gallery exhibit.
- **Restraint Over Noise:** No gratuitous 3D artifacts, heavy gradients, or blinding neon washes. Contrast and micro-glow accents are used deliberately to reward attention and denote state.

### Target Audience & Emotional Impact
Designed to signal senior-level execution to venture-backed founders, design directors, and premium clients. The experience evokes calm competence, architectural discipline, and meticulous craft.

## Colors

The palette leverages an obsidian base with restrained luminescent violet accents and purposeful status highlights.

### Token Architecture
- **Canvas Base (`#0B0812`):** Primary viewport backdrop. Deep, void-like black with subtle violet undertone.
- **Surface Muted (`#120D1C`):** Sub-sections, grouped content wells, and secondary canvas regions.
- **Surface Elevated (`#171121`):** Default card, modal, and interactive container background.
- **Accent Primary (`#9B7BFF`):** Focal actions, interactive emphasis, and controlled ambient glows.
- **Accent Secondary (`#7545E8`):** Deep brand anchoring, active button surfaces, and gradient edge-stops.
- **Success / Status (`#10B981`):** Real-time client availability and live indicators, paired with a soft radial pulsing ring.
- **Text Primary (`#F5F2FA`):** High-contrast off-white for headlines and primary body text.
- **Text Muted (`#B6ADC7`):** Soft violet-slate for secondary copy, meta labels, and descriptions.
- **Stroke Subtle (`#30243F`):** 1px structural outlines defining container silhouettes without harshness.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy prioritizes geometric clarity, strict vertical rhythm, and tight tracking on display headlines to produce an authoritative, technical presence.

### Typographic Rules
- **Font Selection:** Geist serves across all hierarchy tiers for cohesion, paired with JetBrains Mono strictly for technical metadata, tags, and metrics.
- **Headline Rhythm:** Tight negative letter-spacing (`-0.02em` to `-0.035em`) prevents display text from feeling loose, keeping focal project titles dense and punchy.
- **Reading Comfort:** Body copy leverages deliberate line height (`1.6x` to `1.75x`) to preserve effortless scanning against the deep dark canvas.

## Layout & Spacing

A balanced 12-column grid system governs structural alignment, paired with generous vertical section padding to produce an intentional gallery feel.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12 columns, max container width of 1200px, 48px canvas margin, 24px column gutters.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8 columns, 32px canvas margin, 20px column gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 20px canvas margin, 16px column gutters.

### Vertical Rhythm
Major case studies and landing page sections utilize `space-3xl` (104px) spacing intervals, maintaining breathing room between project cards, capability breakdowns, and interactive demonstrations.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through dark surface tiering, subtle violet refraction lines, and soft atmospheric backlights rather than heavy drop shadows.

### Elevation Planes
- **Ground 0 (Base Canvas):** `#0B0812` — Root viewport.
- **Level 1 (Sub-surface / Wells):** `#120D1C` with a 1px border of `#30243F`.
- **Level 2 (Cards & Panels):** `#171121` with a 1px border of `#30243F` and an inner top-edge highlight (`linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(155, 123, 255, 0.08) 0%, transparent 100%)`).
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Floating Nav):** `#171121` with 80% opacity, `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, border `#30243F`, and a subtle diffused ambient shadow: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(11, 8, 18, 0.8), 0 0 24px -4px rgba(155, 123, 255, 0.06)`.

### Glow Accents
Luminescent accents are tightly controlled. On hover states or featured project highlights, an element can emit an accent halo: `0 0 30px -8px rgba(155, 123, 255, 0.22)`. Hard drop shadows and unblended glow halos are strictly forbidden.

## Shapes

The geometric architecture uses restrained, architectural curvature. Corners are soft enough to feel modern and tactile, yet sharp enough to retain technical discipline.

### Corner Radius Mapping
- **Input Fields & Small Buttons:** `0.5rem` (`rounded-md`).
- **Cards, Code Blocks, and Media Containers:** `1rem` (`rounded-lg`).
- **Modals & Hero Feature Displays:** `1.5rem` (`rounded-xl`).
- **Status Badges & Pill Chips:** Full circular radius (`9999px`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#9B7BFF`, text `#0B0812`, font weight `500`. On hover: background `#AA8DFF`, subtle box-shadow `0 0 20px rgba(155, 123, 255, 0.35)`, subtle `translateY(-1px)` transition.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Background `transparent`, text `#F5F2FA`, 1px border `#30243F`. On hover: background `#171121`, border-color `#9B7BFF`.
- **Tertiary Link:** Text `#9B7BFF` with trailing arrow icon; transitions to subtle color brightening on hover.

### Cards & Project Showcase
- **Background:** `#171121` encapsulated by a 1px solid `#30243F` border.
- **Interactive State:** Hover invokes a soft transition (250ms ease-out) shifting border color to `rgba(155, 123, 255, 0.4)` and a soft 20px ambient violet bloom at the base. Images feature subtle `scale(1.02)` zoom containment.

### Status Indicators & Availability Badge
- Pill container (`rounded-full`) with surface `#120D1C`, border `#30243F`, text `#F5F2FA`.
- Contains a 6px circular indicator in `#10B981` flanked by a concentric animated pulse ring (`@keyframes pulse` expanding to 14px with fading opacity from 0.6 to 0).

### Form Inputs
- Background `#120D1C`, 1px border `#30243F`, text `#F5F2FA`, placeholder `#B6ADC7` at 50% opacity.
- **Focus State:** 1px border `#9B7BFF`, zero standard browser outline, complemented by a soft glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(155, 123, 255, 0.12)`.

### Chips & Tech Stack Tags
- Background `rgba(48, 36, 63, 0.45)`, border `1px solid rgba(48, 36, 63, 0.8)`, text `#B6ADC7`, font family `JetBrains Mono`, label-sm scale.
- Compact padding (`0.25rem 0.625rem`) with `0.5rem` border radius.
